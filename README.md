# Orbit: Among the Stars


> Aim for the sun or moon. Even if you fall short, you'll still land among the stars.

Orbit is a spatial productivity companion built for SPECS glasses. It transforms personal goals, projects and daily tasks into an interactive celestial universe.

- Sun — Your biggest ambitions.
- Planets — Projects and areas of focus.
- Stars — Actionable tasks.
- Focus — Timed sessions to help you concentrate.
- Constellation — A visual representation of completed tasks.

Orbit makes progress visible, helping users stay connected to their bigger goals while taking meaningful everyday steps.

## Built With

- Snap Lens Studio
- TypeScript
- Codex
